WHO I AM

I DON'T JUST EDIT.

I believe good editing is more than cutting clips together.

It's about understanding the story, choosing the right moments, creating the right rhythm and making every second of the video worth watching.

Whether it's a short Reel, a YouTube video or a cinematic project, my goal is always the same: make the content better than it was when it started.

WHAT I DO

FROM RAW TO FINAL.

I work on short-form content, YouTube videos, cinematic edits and motion graphics.

My editing style focuses on clean storytelling, engaging pacing, strong visuals, sound design and attention to detail.

I'm constantly learning new techniques and improving my skills so I can create better work with every project.
